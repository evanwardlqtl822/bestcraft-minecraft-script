## What BestCraft Does

BestCraft acts as a Minecraft-oriented AI reference assistant. Its chat interface lets players retrieve useful gameplay information without interrupting their session, from a specific crafting recipe to mob advice or a redstone explanation.

Information is supplied through an on-device knowledge base, making the script suited to repeated lookups during exploration, construction, or combat preparation. Its current scope covers frequently used Minecraft recipes, strategies, and building-related guidance with an emphasis on quick access.

## Minecraft Compatibility

BestCraft is intended for Minecraft gameplay reference. It is not designed for editing worlds or automating gameplay. The quality and relevance of its answers depend on the content available in the local knowledge base and the Minecraft version in use.

The script is geared toward common survival, building, and progression questions. Highly specialized modded configurations may receive less relevant results. If you use a custom launcher or client, make sure the BestCraft files are installed in the location required by that workflow.
